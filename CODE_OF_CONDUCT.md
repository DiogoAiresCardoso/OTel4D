# Community conduct

Discuss technical choices with evidence. Critique the change, welcome questions, credit contributors and respect personal boundaries. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable.

Contact the repository maintainer through an established private channel to report an incident. If you need a channel, ask the maintainer for a private conversation without posting incident details. A maintainer with a conflict should ask another trusted reviewer to handle the report with the reporter's consent. Restrict access to the people handling the case. Possible responses include a warning, removal of harmful content and restricted participation.
